A little more about us

Our Little Cat Cafe

Good coffee, gentle company, and a sunny place to slow down in Lethbridge.

A cafe with room for whiskers

Neko Atsume Cafe is a cozy neighborhood stop for coffee lovers and cat people alike. Come in for a warm drink, settle into a comfortable corner, and let the cats set the pace.

The cats come first

Every cat has their own favorite perch, pace, and way of saying hello. We keep the cafe calm and give our resident cats plenty of choice and space, so a good visit always starts with treating them gently.

Some of our cats are looking for their next home, while others are happy as permanent cafe residents. Visit Our Cats to meet the current crew and learn a little about each one.

Made for lingering

There is no rush here. Bring a friend, take a quiet break, or join us for one of the small community events on our calendar. We hope you leave with a favorite cat, a favorite drink, or both.
